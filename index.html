Most brands spend years trying to close the gap between how they present themselves and how the market sees them. Sometimes the market's view is the better one, and the smart move is to adopt it. Volvo built sturdy cars for hard Swedish roads; people came to see them as safe, and Volvo made safety its brand. Guests already see Dakshinayan as a place to return to. We would build on that rather than invent something new.

Our starting recommendation

Be the regulars' place, in every outlet and every new city.

Talk to regulars first

Their stories, their usual orders and their tables carry the brand into neighbourhoods that don't know it yet.

Win the everyday

Habits form on ordinary weekdays, not only at weekends. Every outlet should give people a reason to come back on a Tuesday.

Open every outlet the same way

One opening playbook, adapted to each neighbourhood and city, and measured on the same numbers each time.

A queue is a launch. A regular is a business.Even in a new city, Dakshinayan doesn't arrive as a newcomer. It arrives with two decades of regulars behind it.
